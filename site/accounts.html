$10.00 vs $7.00 per lot

Per lot, ECN usually costs less. Standard exists because it is simpler: one number, no math, and you can start with $10. Both are honest. Pick the one that fits how you think, not how much you have.

Three dollars per lot is about $60 a month if you trade 20 lots. And we earn about the same on either account, so we have no reason to steer you. We would rather just show you the math.

Spreads are floating. The math above uses each account's starting spread; when the market gets busy, both totals rise together.

Some brokers quote commission per side, $3.50 each way. We quote the whole trip: $7 per lot.

Asked before you asked.

Which account should I choose?

Standard keeps every cost inside one number; ECN usually works out cheaper if you trade often and count your costs. There is no account we quietly steer you into. The demo is free, so decide with your hands instead of your head.

What is the difference between Standard and ECN?

Standard folds our cost into a wider spread with no commission. ECN shows the raw market spread and charges a visible $7 per lot instead. You pay us either way. The difference is where you can see it.

Who is the Precious Metals account for?

People and businesses who hold physical gold and want to protect its value: gold shops, jewellers, dealers. Your gold sits still; its price does not. Leverage is capped at 1:10 on purpose, because hedging is protection, not acceleration.

Is the demo really free, or is there a countdown?

Yes. Real prices, virtual funds, no deposit, and no countdown clock nudging you to go live. Some demos expire so you will deposit sooner; ours does not. Take months if you need them.

Do you offer swap-free or Islamic accounts?

Not today. Standard and ECN carry standard overnight swaps, and the Precious Metals account is swap-free for the first 5 days of every position, counted date to date. Some swap-free labels just move the fee somewhere quieter. We would rather tell you no than dress a fee up as a feature.

Am I stuck with the account type I pick?

No. An existing account cannot be converted, but you can open another type anytime. Hold both, compare them, keep what works.

Do you make money when I lose?

No. PCX is A-Book: your orders go to the real market through many liquidity providers, and we earn from spreads and commissions when you trade, not from your losses. The Why A-Book page explains why we chose this model.
