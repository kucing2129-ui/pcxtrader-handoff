Privacy Policy

At PCXTrader, we are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your personal data with transparency and care. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal information when you visit our website or engage with our services.

By using our website and services, you agree to the terms outlined in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect#

When you interact with PCXTrader, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, contact number, date of birth, address, and other identifying information.
  • Financial Information: Bank account details, credit/debit card numbers, and transaction history.
  • Trading Information: Account activity, deposits, withdrawals, trades executed, and platform usage data.
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, operating system, and device identifiers collected via cookies or similar technologies.

2. How We Use Your Information#

PCXTrader uses your personal data to:

  • Open and manage your trading account
  • Process transactions and payments
  • Verify your identity and meet regulatory obligations
  • Provide support and respond to inquiries
  • Send service-related notifications and promotional content (only with your consent)
  • Improve our website, services, and user experience

3. Sharing and Disclosure#

We do not sell or rent your personal data. However, we may share your information in the following circumstances:

  • With Service Providers: For operational purposes such as payment processing, customer verification, and IT services.
  • With Regulatory Authorities: To comply with legal requirements, court orders, or regulatory inquiries.
  • Within Our Group: With affiliated companies and business partners bound by confidentiality agreements.

4. Data Security#

PCXTrader implements robust technical and organizational measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. While no system is completely secure, we follow industry best practices to safeguard your personal information.

5. Cookies and Tracking Technologies#

Our website uses cookies to enhance functionality and gather analytics. You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings, but disabling them may affect certain website features.

6. Your Rights#

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights:

  • Access and review your personal data
  • Request correction or deletion
  • Object to data processing or withdraw consent
  • File a complaint with a data protection authority

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at compliance@pcxtrader.com.

7. Third-Party Links#

Our website may contain links to external sites not operated by PCXTrader. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those websites.

8. Changes to This Policy#

PCXTrader reserves the right to modify this Privacy Policy at any time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. We encourage users to review this policy regularly.

Contact Us#
